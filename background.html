Record of Contribution

Brandon M. Whittiker served as the primary human contributor to the Identity Panic Toolkit project.
His role included originating the core concepts, defining the ethical and functional scope of the project, drafting and revising written materials, and establishing standards for tone, accessibility, and non-coercive use. He directed the development process, made all final editorial and judgment decisions, and ensured that the materials remained focused on de-escalation, psychosocial education, and public availability.
Throughout the project, AI tools were used in a supportive capacity; however, the conceptual direction, content selection, refinement, and release decisions were determined by human evaluation and intent. Mr. Whittiker also determined the distribution approach, including the decision to release the Identity Panic Toolkit freely, without copyright restriction or organizational ownership, to allow unrestricted sharing, adaptation, and reuse.
This record reflects sustained human effort over time rather than automated generation or incidental contribution.
